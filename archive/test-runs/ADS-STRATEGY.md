## Platform Selection Analysis

### Budget Reality

Your $1,000/month budget is below the standard e-commerce minimum ($3,000+ across Google + Meta). This means you must be strategic — **one platform done well beats three done poorly**.

| Platform | Min Viable Budget | Fit for Your Niche | Verdict |
|----------|------------------|--------------------|---------|
| **Meta Ads** | $600-800/mo | Excellent — visual products, community targeting, ASC | **Launch first** |
| **TikTok Ads** | $300/mo ($50/day min) | Strong — climbing is visual, UGC-native, cheap CPMs | **Add at $1,500+/mo** |
| Google PMax | $1,000/mo | Good but needs your full budget alone | Wait until $2,000+/mo |
| LinkedIn Ads | $3,000/mo | Not relevant for DTC merch | Skip |
| Microsoft Ads | $200-300/mo | Low search volume for niche | Not yet |

### Recommended Platform Sequence

```
Phase 1 (Months 1-3):  Meta Ads         → $1,000/mo (100%)
Phase 2 (Months 4-6):  Meta + TikTok    → $800 + $500 ($1,300/mo if budget grows)
Phase 3 (Months 7-12): Meta + TikTok + Google → $3,000+ total
```

---

## Why Meta First

1. **Best DTC platform**: 50-68% of e-commerce ad spend goes to Meta for a reason
2. **Visual products win**: Chalk clouds, brushing holds, climbing lifestyle — perfect for feed content
3. **Interest targeting**: Rock climbing, bouldering, indoor climbing, specific brands (La Sportiva, Petzl, Black Diamond, Metolius)
4. **Advantage+ Shopping**: Median ROAS of 4.52 — best-performing campaign type
5. **Retargeting**: Build website visitor audiences from day 1
6. **Lower barrier**: No product feed required to start (unlike Google Shopping)

## Competitive Landscape

### What Climbing Brands Do on Meta/TikTok
Most climbing merch/chalk brands are **under-advertised** on Meta — this is your opportunity:
- Major brands (Black Diamond, Metolius) focus on retail/wholesale, not DTC ads
- Niche DTC brands (Friction Labs, Psychi, 8B+) run Meta but often with limited creative
- **Gap**: Authentic community-driven content is rare — most run product shots

### Your Competitive Advantage
- Niche community that identifies strongly with the sport
- Products are consumable (chalk) = repeat purchases = high LTV
- UGC is easy to create — anyone at the gym can film

### Kill Rules
- **3x Kill Rule**: If an ad/ad set spends 3x your target CPA with zero conversions, pause immediately
- **Creative kill**: If a creative spends $20-30 with no purchases, replace it
- **20% Scale Rule**: Only increase budget by 20% at a time, wait 3-5 days between increases

## Risk Factors & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Budget too low for learning phase | Can't exit "Learning Limited" | Consolidate to 1-2 ad sets max; optimize for AddToCart if Purchase volume too low |
| Creative fatigue at low spend | Audience exhaustion faster | Refresh creative every 3-4 weeks; have 4-6 creatives ready at all times |
| Seasonal climbing demand | Winter indoor = different messaging than summer outdoor | Plan seasonal creative calendar |
| iOS tracking limitations | 30-40% data loss without CAPI | Set up CAPI from day 1 (non-negotiable) |
| Amazon cannibalization | Customers buy on Amazon instead of DTC | Offer DTC-exclusive products/bundles; build email list |
